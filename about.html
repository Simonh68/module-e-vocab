About E-Vocab for Module E

E-Vocab for Module E is a free, responsive learning application for vocabulary learning and practice for the Module E Bagrut on computers, tablets and phones.

The project

The application follows the Ministry of Education Lists A–D. Each official list is shuffled internally and divided into three manageable teaching groups, producing twelve online activities from A1 to D3.

The edited database contains separate records for different grammatical roles, POS-specific examples, learner-friendly meaning support and relevant Family Members. The activities and master workbook are maintained as synchronized versions of the same 982 learning records.

Creator and editor

I am Simon Halevi, an English teacher and independent pedagogical developer with approximately twenty-two years of experience teaching English. I created and edited this project independently to give teachers and students a structured, practical and reusable way to study Module E vocabulary in manageable stages.

Teacher presentations, Bagrut-compatible assessments, separate answer keys and scoring software are planned as a complementary extension to this learning unit. They are not currently available and are not included in the content unit submitted at this stage.
